# Contributing to Momiji

Momiji is currently working through its data model. Please discuss substantial
changes, especially schema changes, before implementing them. Small corrections
are welcome directly.

## Rights and licensing

Submit only material you have the right to contribute. Obtain any necessary
permission from your employer or another rights holder before submitting it.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted for
inclusion are under Apache-2.0, as described in Section 5 of [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Flag any proposed exception before the contribution is accepted.

Identify third-party material, its source, and its license. Preserve required
copyright, license, and attribution notices, and use only material whose terms
are compatible with its intended inclusion and distribution in Momiji. If the
rights are unclear, raise the question before submitting the material.

There is initially no separate contributor license agreement or required DCO
sign-off. Contributors retain ownership of their contributions; contributing
does not assign copyright to the maintainer.

## Design and review

Explain the problem, the proposed behavior, and how you checked the change.
For model changes, include worked examples covering relevant state transitions
and explain compatibility with existing data. Do not silently migrate stored data
or discard fields a client does not understand.

Keep proposals focused. Submission does not guarantee acceptance or a commitment
to maintain a proposed feature.
